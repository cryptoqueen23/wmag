A well-kept home is not a decorated one. It is a considered one — the kind where a guest is handed a glass before they finish taking off their coat, where the good napkins get used on a Tuesday, where nothing is too precious to actually use.

None of this requires money. It requires attention, and a willingness to treat the home as a craft worth practicing rather than a problem to be outsourced.

Start small: one drawer that always has matches and candles. One shelf that always has something to offer a guest. One habit — five minutes before bed — of resetting the rooms you'll wake up into.
