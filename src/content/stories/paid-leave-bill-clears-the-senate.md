The Senate voted 61-39 on Thursday to advance a bill establishing twelve weeks of federally guaranteed paid family leave, sending the measure to a House-Senate conference committee tasked with reconciling differences between the two chambers' versions.

Seven senators crossed party lines to support the bill. The conference committee has 30 days to produce a unified text before it returns for a final vote in both chambers.
