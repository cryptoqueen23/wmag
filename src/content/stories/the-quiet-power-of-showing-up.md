Every tradition we admire from the outside — a marriage of forty years, a career built over decades, a faith practiced daily rather than performed occasionally — looks, from the inside, less like inspiration and more like repetition.

Nobody puts "showed up on the unremarkable Tuesdays" on a highlight reel. But that's the actual mechanism. Consistency is not a personality trait. It's a series of small, boring decisions, made again and again, long after the feeling that started them has worn off.
