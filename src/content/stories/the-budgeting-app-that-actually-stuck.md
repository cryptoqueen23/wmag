Most budgeting apps fail for the same reason diets fail: they ask you to change your behavior instead of meeting you where you already are.

This one won by doing less. No category-guessing, no guilt notifications, just a single number — what's actually safe to spend this week — updated automatically from linked accounts.

**What works:** the weekly number, the total absence of judgmental push notifications, a genuinely useful shared-household mode.

**What doesn't:** the investment tracking is an afterthought, and the free tier caps you at two accounts, which won't cover most households.

**Verdict:** worth the $6 a month if you've bounced off three other apps already. Skip it if you're happy with a spreadsheet.
