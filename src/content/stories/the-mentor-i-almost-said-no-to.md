Rosa Villanueva was six years into hospital chaplaincy when a former professor called about a hospice startup that needed a director of patient care. "I almost let it go to voicemail," she says. "I had a whole plan, and it didn't include this."

She took the call anyway, then the job, then built the organization that now serves eleven counties.

"The best career advice I ever got," she says, "was to stop treating the plan as more real than the person standing in front of me."
