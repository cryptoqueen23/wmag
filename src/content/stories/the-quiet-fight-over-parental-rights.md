Several states are considering legislation this session that would require schools to notify parents before changing how a child is addressed in class, or before a minor accesses certain counseling services. Supporters call it a matter of parental authority; opponents call it government overreach into private family and medical decisions.

Here is what the bills actually say, state by state, what they don't say, and where the real disagreement lies — not in the headlines, but in the fine print of who is notified, when, and under what exceptions.
