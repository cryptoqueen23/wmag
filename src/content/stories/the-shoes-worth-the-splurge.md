A shopping edit lives and dies on specifics, so here they are: the loafer that doesn't need breaking in, the boot that survives a real winter, the flat you can stand in for a twelve-hour shift.

We tested each pair for at least a month before writing a word.

**The everyday loafer** — supple from the first wear, holds up to a commute.

**The winter boot** — actually waterproof, not "water resistant."

**The going-out flat** — dressy enough for dinner, sane enough to walk home in.

**The white sneaker** — stays white longer than seems reasonable.

**The one splurge** — a bag, not a shoe, but it earned its place on this list anyway.
