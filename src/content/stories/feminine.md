There is a difference between being told what a woman should be and becoming curious about what womanhood can hold.

Across style, home, work, family life and culture, women are revisiting traditions once dismissed as frivolous or old-fashioned. The interesting part is not nostalgia. It is choice.

Femininity can be soft without being weak, beautiful without being decorative, traditional without being passive, and ambitious without treating womanhood as an obstacle to overcome.
